Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how ShiftFile Tools handles data.

Data Handling

ShiftFile Tools processes all files locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit your files to any server. The only data that may be sent to external servers is the jsPDF library when you use the Images to PDF tool, and that is fetched from a CDN over HTTPS.

No Tracking

We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics. We do not embed third-party analytics scripts. The only local storage we use is a single key to remember your theme preference (light or dark).

GDPR Compliance

Because we do not collect personal data, GDPR obligations are minimal. You have the right to request deletion of any data we might hold, though we hold none. Contact us at privacy@shiftfile.tools.

Children's Privacy

Our tools are safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
