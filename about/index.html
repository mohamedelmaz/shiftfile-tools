About ShiftFile Tools

Your files, your device, your privacy.

Our Story

ShiftFile Tools was built with a simple belief: file conversion should be fast, free, and private. We saw too many tools that required uploads, accounts, or paid plans. We decided to build something different — a suite of 14 in-browser tools that work offline and never touch a server.

Our Philosophy

Privacy is not a feature. It is the default. Every tool in ShiftFile runs entirely in your browser using vanilla JavaScript. No telemetry, no cookies for tracking, no third-party scripts (except the optional jsPDF CDN for the PDF tool).

Open Source

We believe in transparency. The code is open source under the MIT license. You can audit it, fork it, or contribute. See our Developers page for the SDK and integration guide.
