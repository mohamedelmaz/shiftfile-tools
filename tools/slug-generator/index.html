Frequently Asked Questions

Which characters are transliterated?
Common Latin accents are mapped: é→e, à→a, ñ→n, ß→ss, and more.
What happens with max length?
The slug is truncated and trailing dashes are removed to keep it clean.
Does it support non-Latin scripts?
Non-Latin characters are stripped, leaving only ASCII letters and numbers.
