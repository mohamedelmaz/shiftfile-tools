Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the two modes?
Component mode encodes special characters like spaces. Full URL mode preserves reserved URL characters like / and :.
Can I decode a URL?
Use the Full URL mode to decode, or replace the input with the encoded string and choose the opposite mode.
Are my URLs uploaded?
No. Encoding and decoding happen entirely in your browser.
