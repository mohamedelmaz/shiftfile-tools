Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize by percentage instead of pixels?
Yes, enter any pixel value; the tool preserves the original aspect ratio by default.
Does resizing reduce image quality?
We use high-quality smoothing, but enlarging beyond the original dimensions will reduce detail.
Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens locally in your browser.
