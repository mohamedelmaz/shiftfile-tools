Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose 2x or 4x scale?
Higher scale factors produce larger PNGs suitable for high-DPI screens and print.
Does this support embedded fonts?
Fonts referenced in SVGs may not render if they are not installed locally. Convert paths to outlines for best results.
Are my files uploaded?
No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser.
