Frequently Asked Questions

Does Image Converter support AVIF or HEIC?
No. AVIF and HEIC are not supported because browser support is inconsistent. Use PNG, JPG, or WebP for reliable results.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
Can I convert multiple images at once?
Currently, Image Converter handles one image at a time. Use Image Compressor for batch processing.
