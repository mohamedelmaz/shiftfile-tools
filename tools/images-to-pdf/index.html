Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, and WebP are supported and automatically converted to JPEG for PDF embedding.
Is there a page limit?
No hard limit, but very large batches may be slower depending on your device.
Are my images uploaded?
No. Everything happens locally in your browser.
