Frequently Asked Questions

Which formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, and WebP input. Output can be WebP or JPG for best compression results.
Does batch compression affect quality?
You control the quality slider. Lower quality means smaller files but more compression artifacts.
Is there a limit on the number of images?
No hard limit. Performance depends on your browser and device memory.
